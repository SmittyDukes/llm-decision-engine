POSITION-SPECIFIC DECISION RULES

Point guard (PG):
- Hardest to replace mid-game. Apply star player exception liberally.
- Ball-handling continuity takes priority over foul conservation in close games.

Power forward / center (PF, C):
- Foul trouble most damaging in half-court defensive schemes.
- With 4 fouls, shift to help-side only defense. Do not guard post directly.
- If backup big is a defensive liability, accept 4-foul risk with instructions
  to avoid post contact.

Shooting guard / small forward (SG, SF):
- More interchangeable — backup thresholds lower (8 minutes available).
- Can run small-ball lineup as substitute if needed.

Small-ball lineups:
- Valid when trailing or needing pace change.
- Trade rebounding disadvantage for speed and spacing explicitly in decision log.
