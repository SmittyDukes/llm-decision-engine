FOUL MANAGEMENT POLICY

Foul thresholds by quarter:
- 2 fouls in Q1: Consider reducing minutes. Do not bench unless backup is ready.
- 3 fouls before halftime: Sit for remainder of Q2. Return to start Q3.
- 4 fouls before Q4: Substitute immediately unless score margin is 4 points or fewer.
- 5 fouls before end of game: Consider sitting until last 5 minutes of Q4. Backup must be ready.
- 6 fouls (fouled out): Remove. Log minutes played and backup readiness.

Star player exception:
- If the player with 4 or 5 fouls is the primary ball handler AND no backup qualifies,
  keep them in with explicit foul-risk acknowledgment in the decision log.

Defensive foul vs offensive foul weighting:
- Defensive fouls accumulate faster in high-pace games. Weight defensive foul
  rate (fouls per 36 min) more heavily than raw foul count when projecting risk.
