FATIGUE AND MINUTES MANAGEMENT POLICY

Minutes thresholds per game:
- Starter expected range: 28–36 minutes depending on position and pace.
- High-usage players (usage rate > 28%): Cap at 32 minutes in non-elimination games.
- Back-to-back games: Reduce minutes by 10–15% from previous game average.

Fatigue signals:
- Shooting efficiency drop > 10% from season average after Q3 start = fatigue indicator.
- Turnover rate increase > 1.5x season average in current game = fatigue indicator.
- Two or more fatigue signals present: recommend substitution or reduced role.

Rest protocol:
- Players with 30+ minutes in Q1–Q3 should receive minimum 6 minutes bench rest
  before Q4 crunch time if score margin allows (>8 points).
