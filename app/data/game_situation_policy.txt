GAME SITUATION DECISION POLICY

Score margin categories:
- Comfortable: margin > 15 points
- Competitive: margin 8–14 points
- Close: margin < 8 points
- Deficit: losing by any amount

Close game overrides:
- In close games (margin < 8), standard foul thresholds are relaxed by 1 foul.
- In close games, fatigue policy rest windows are reduced — skip bench rest
  unless player has 38+ minutes.

Clutch time definition:
- Q4 with under 5 minutes, margin < 8 points.
- Clutch time: only use players with positive +/- in current game or season
  clutch rating above team average.

Comeback protocol:
- Trailing by 15+ in Q4: high-pace lineup, accept foul risk, maximize possessions.
- Do not rest key players during comeback attempts regardless of minutes.
