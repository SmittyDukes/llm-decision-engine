SUBSTITUTION DECISION RULES

Timing windows (preferred substitution moments):
- Dead ball situations only. Never substitute mid-possession.
- After a free throw attempt — clock is stopped, clean transition.
- Timeout calls are primary substitution opportunities.

Rotation depth requirements:
- Backup must have minimum 10 minutes available (not in foul trouble).
- If primary backup is also in foul trouble, escalate to third-string with
  explicit risk flag in decision log.

Lineup continuity rule:
- Do not substitute more than 2 players simultaneously unless trailing by 10+.
- Preserve at least 2 starters on court at all times during non-garbage-time.

Garbage time threshold:
- Lead of 20+ points with under 5 minutes: full bench rotation appropriate.
- Lead of 12–19 points with under 3 minutes: begin staggered rest.
